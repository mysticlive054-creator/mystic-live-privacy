Mystic Live — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Mystic Live ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our social, live-streaming, messaging, content-sharing and AI assistance features.

Information we process

Depending on the features you use, this may include your account identifiers, email address or phone number, profile details, photos, videos, audio, messages, posts, comments, live-stream activity, virtual coin and reward transactions, app interactions, search activity, device identifiers, diagnostic information, and content you submit to Mystic AI. Some information is provided by you; other information is generated when you use the service.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate live streaming and communications, display user-generated content, manage virtual transactions and rewards, prevent abuse, review reports, provide support, improve reliability and personalize the experience where applicable.

Mystic AI

When you use Mystic AI, your questions and related technical information may be processed to generate responses, maintain service safety, troubleshoot problems and improve assistance. Depending on the active implementation, questions or related records may be stored in our backend and made available to authorized administrators for review. Please do not enter passwords, payment credentials or highly sensitive personal information into AI questions. We will update this notice as AI recording, retention and service integrations are verified or changed.

Service providers and sharing

We use infrastructure and service providers, including Google Firebase and Google authentication services, to operate app features. Other integrated providers may process information as necessary to provide live communications, payments or AI functionality. We may disclose information where legally required, to protect users or prevent fraud. We do not describe data as never shared: transfers to service providers are assessed according to their roles and applicable Google Play disclosure rules.

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including secure connections where supported. No system can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained as needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and protect users. Retention varies by data type and purpose.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can request deletion of your Mystic Live account and associated personal data by emailing admin@mysticlive.in with the subject “Mystic Live Account Deletion Request”. Include your Mystic Live user ID and a way for us to verify account ownership. Do not email your password. We will review the request and explain any information that must be retained for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons. You may also contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of specific personal information, subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

User content and safety

Users must not upload or broadcast unlawful, abusive, hateful, sexually explicit or exploitative content. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation, including child sexual abuse material. Users can report content or accounts through available reporting features. Reports may be reviewed by authorized moderators, and we may take enforcement action and cooperate with lawful requests.

Age requirements

Mystic Live is intended for adults aged 18 and above. People under 18 must not create an account or use the service.

Updates

We may revise this policy as our features and data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Privacy, safety and deletion inquiries: admin@mysticlive.in.
